How to Play?

Your mission is to transport every passenger safely. Plan carefully and keep the roads clear!

Choose your buses and send them along their predefined routes. Match each bus with passengers of the same color and make sure there are enough seats for the whole passenger group. Plan the order carefully, avoid traffic and transport all passengers to complete the level and unlock the next district. You have 3 shared lives for your entire journey!
